The Challenge

Clinica Sorriso had been open for four years and relied entirely on word of mouth. Their Google Business Profile had 31 reviews, 14 unanswered, duplicate listings on two major directories, and photos from opening day. When nearby patients searched "dentist near me", they never appeared — Google showed the same three competitors every time.

The owner was spending €400 a month on ads to compensate, with no idea which clicks became patients.

What We Did

  • Rebuilt the Google Business Profile: accurate categories, service areas, opening hours, and fresh photos updated monthly.
  • Cleaned up inconsistent name, address, and phone details across the directories Google trusts most.
  • Set up a simple review request system that asked happy patients at the right moment — reviews grew from 31 to 120+ in three months.
  • Rewrote the website's key pages around the exact terms patients actually search for, like "emergency dentist Lisbon".
  • Tracked calls and direction requests weekly, so every change could be tied to real patient inquiries.

The Results

By week six, Clinica Sorriso had entered the local map pack for their main searches. By week twelve, they held the #1 position for "dentist Lisbon" and appeared in the top three for eleven related searches. Calls and direction requests from Google more than tripled, and the paid ads they had been using as a crutch were switched off for good.

Most importantly, the front desk stopped wondering where new patients came from. Google became their most reliable source — and it keeps working without a monthly ad budget.

"We went from hoping people heard about us to being the first name they saw. The phone rings every day now, and we're not paying for a single click."
— Practice Manager, Clinica Sorriso, Lisbon
